nouvil

Where the river meets the mountains

Scroll slowly and watch the night come alive. The moon rises, the mountains drift past at different speeds, and a small boat sails across the river under a sky full of stars.


Every layer of this page moves a little differently. That difference creates depth, the same effect called parallax, and it is built with only HTML, CSS and a few lines of JavaScript that listen to your scroll.


Nouvil is a practice project made to explore how a simple scroll can turn flat pictures into a scene. Take your time, scroll back up, and watch it all move again.
